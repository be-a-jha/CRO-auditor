          Care & Delivery

          Care Guide
          Wipe your coffee table regularly with a soft, dry cloth, using a lightly dampened one only when needed. Keep it away from direct sunlight, heat, and moisture to prevent fading or warping. Use coasters or mats under hot or cold items to protect the surface, and avoid dragging objects across it. Check fittings occasionally to ensure everything stays stable and secure.

          Buying Guide
          Our coffee tables often feature natural materials like wood, stone, marble, or metal, so small variations in grain, colour, and texture are perfectly normal. Lighting and screen settings may also create slight colour differences. These natural characteristics aren’t flaws—they’re what make each FableRoom piece feel unique and beautifully crafted.
